## Global Climate & Energy Analysis and Prediction
### Project Overview

Python (3.14.6 or later)

This project analyzes global climate and energy data to explore relationships between environmental conditions, energy consumption, renewable energy adoption, and CO₂ emissions.

Using Python, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), data visualization, and machine learning, the project aims to identify key patterns, understand influencing factors, and develop predictive models for environmental and energy-related indicators.

### Project Objectives
Analyze climate and energy trends across different countries.
Explore relationships between temperature, humidity, and energy consumption.
Investigate factors influencing CO₂ emissions.
Understand the impact of renewable energy on environmental indicators.
Identify important features using statistical and machine learning techniques.
Build predictive models and generate data-driven insights.
### Dataset Description

The dataset contains 10 features covering climate, environmental, and energy indicators.

Feature	Description
date	Observation date
country	Country name
avg_temperature	Average temperature
humidity	Humidity level
co2_emission	CO₂ emissions
energy_consumption	Energy consumption
renewable_share	Percentage of renewable energy
urban_population	Urban population percentage
industrial_activity_index	Industrial activity level
energy_price	Energy price
### Tools & Technologies
Python: Data analysis and programming
Pandas & NumPy: Data cleaning and manipulation
Matplotlib & Seaborn: Data visualization
Scikit-learn: Machine learning and model evaluation
Jupyter Notebook: Interactive analysis
### Project Workflow
Data Understanding: Explore dataset structure and statistical summaries.
Data Cleaning: Handle missing values, duplicates, and outliers.
Exploratory Data Analysis: Identify climate and energy patterns.
Data Visualization: Create trend charts, distributions, and correlation heatmaps.
Feature Engineering: Prepare relevant features for modeling.
Machine Learning: Apply regression algorithms such as Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost.
Model Evaluation: Evaluate performance using MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R².
### Expected Outcomes
Discover climate and energy consumption patterns.
Identify factors associated with CO₂ emissions.
Understand relationships between renewable energy and environmental indicators.
Predict energy consumption or CO₂ emissions.
Generate meaningful insights to support environmental and energy decision-making.
